DHA 6-Month PED Wait & COC Waiver

Under Dubai DHA rules, declared pre-existing & chronic conditions (PED) carry a standard 6-month waiting period. This is waived (0 days) if you submit a valid Certificate of Continuity (COC) with ≤ 30 days coverage gap.

COC Gap Limit: ≤ 30 Days

UAE BMI Underwriting Rules

BMI 18.5–24.9 qualifies for standard rates & instant issuance. BMI ≥ 30 triggers +15% to +45% medical loading, a mandatory Medical Application Form (MAF), and HbA1c/Lipid lab reports before binding.

BMI ≥ 35: No Instant Binding

Portal Document Restrictions

Your Visa Emirate must match the policy regulator (Dubai=DHA, Abu Dhabi=DoH). Visit/Tourist visas cannot buy resident plans. EBP plans strictly require a MOHRE Labour Contract / Salary Certificate (≤ AED 4,000).

Strict ICP / DHA Verification

Nationality-Based Actuarial Pricing

In the UAE, insurer quotes vary by nationality due to home-country repatriation/elective networks, regional claims frequency, and US/European billing exposure. Accurate nationality consent is required upfront.

    How to Use Your UAE Health Insurance, Key Benefits & Underwriting Rules

    A step-by-step handbook to using your Emirates ID at UAE hospitals, maximizing benefits, and understanding Dubai DHA PED, BMI, and portal document regulations.

    1. How to Use Your UAE Health Insurance Step-by-Step (Post-Purchase)

    Follow these 6 steps from Day 1 to ensure smooth cashless approvals and zero rejected claims.

    STEP 1 • ACTIVATION

    Activate & Link to Emirates ID

    In the UAE, your Emirates ID IS your cashless medical card. Once issued, your insurer electronically links your policy to ICP/DHA eClaimLink. Always carry your EID or UAE Pass digital EID.

    STEP 2 • NETWORK CHECK

    Verify Your Exact TPA Network

    Before booking an appointment, check that the clinic/hospital is inside your exact TPA tier (e.g., NextCare RN3 vs. Basic). In-network visits use Direct Billing; out-of-network requires paying upfront and claiming reimbursement.

    STEP 3 • OUTPATIENT & PHARMACY

    Outpatient Visits & Co-Pays

    At reception, you only pay your policy co-pay (e.g., 0%, 10%, or 20% capped at AED 50–100). Prescribed formulary medicines are dispensed at network pharmacies with your applicable pharmacy co-pay.

    STEP 4 • PRE-AUTHORIZATION

    Pre-Approval via DHA eClaimLink

    MRI/CT scans, physiotherapy, and planned surgeries require hospital-submitted pre-authorization: ~6 hours for elective outpatient diagnostics, 24 hours for elective inpatient admission, and immediate for true emergencies.

    STEP 5 • REIMBURSEMENT

    Submitting a Reimbursement Claim

    If treated out-of-network or abroad, submit within 30–90 days: (1) Itemized Tax Invoice & Payment Receipt, (2) Stamped Medical Report / Discharge Summary, (3) Doctor's Prescription, and (4) UAE Bank IBAN.

    STEP 6 • RENEWAL & COC

    Job Change & Renewal Protection

    When switching employers or insurers, always request your Certificate of Continuity (COC) and bind your new policy within 30 days so your 6-month PED waiting period never resets.
